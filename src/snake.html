'==== Prog "SNACKI" ====
"SPEED 1 To 10"?→S
'=====INIT==========

1→P

RanInt#(2,6)→N
27→R
27→Z
If S≤10
Then Int (80÷S)→S
Else 1→S
IfEnd
Locate 1,1,"○"
Locate 2,1,"●"
Locate M,N,"¥"
'=====MAIN LOOP=====
While 1
'=====DELAY&KEY=====
For 1→I To S
GetKey→T

T=53⇒27→T
(T=28) And (Z≠37)⇒T→R
(T=37) And (Z≠28)⇒T→R
(T=38) And (Z≠27)⇒T→R
(T=27) And (Z≠38)⇒T→R
Next

'Mat B[X,Y]→Z
'=====MOVE==========
MOD(P,148)+1→P

Z=38⇒X-1→X
Z=27⇒X+1→X

If X=M And Y=N
Then

RanInt#(2,6)→N

WhileEnd
